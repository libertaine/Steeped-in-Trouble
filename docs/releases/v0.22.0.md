# 0.22.0 — The Training Cup

The spoon has started taking apprentices. The leaves in the practice cup are
real. The consequences are not.

- **The Training Cup:** an optional, replayable five-stage tutorial, offered from
  the title screen, the Field Guide and the Tea Break. Move the spoon, fire steam,
  pour milk, launch sugar, then clear a short practice wave. Every stage uses the
  game's actual movement, weapons and collisions, so steam fired into an empty
  lane earns only experience. There is no time limit, and mistakes cost no lives.
- **What happens in training stays in training:** it runs apart from your saved
  pot, records and normal progression, and it sends no analytics events. Start
  Playing takes you back to your existing pot, exactly where you left it.
- **One layout:** Full View has retired. The former Compact layout is now the only
  presentation on desktop, tablet and phone, and it adapts to the window. The view
  switches are gone, and old view links still load normally.
- **Room to choose:** Day and Tea Shortage choices stay readable and clickable in
  narrow and short windows. Required choices scroll within their own space, narrow
  Day cards stack, and the action dock has stopped sitting on the small print.
- **Analytics that know their place:** the analytics module now loads separately
  from the game, so a privacy extension that blocks it cannot stop the cup from
  brewing. Do Not Track and `?analytics=off` still keep the tracker off, and the
  events it can send have not changed. See
  [how the counting works](../ANALYTICS.md).

Normal gameplay is unchanged, and earlier saved pots continue.

Automated browser tests cover the Training Cup's mechanics and layouts. They
cannot say whether a new player finds it clear, or how it feels on a physical
phone.
