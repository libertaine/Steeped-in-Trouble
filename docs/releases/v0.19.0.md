# 0.19.0 — The Cup Has Gone Fungal

There is something growing in the tea. It has a manager. The spoon would
like to speak to someone else.

**[▶ Play it in your browser](https://libertaine.github.io/Steeped-in-Trouble/)**

- **Mushroom Tea:** meet puffballs, sprouts and spore clouds in a new tea
  that joins the rotation after the Mycelium Manager opens Chapter 3.
- **Strange Happenings:** Time Rewind, Breathing Cup and Impostor Spoon
  bring three theatrical surprises to Mushroom Tea. Their schedule follows
  your Mushroom Tea cups and survives a saved-pot restore.
- **The Mycelium Manager:** cut back roots with steam, freeze them with
  milk, clear lanes with sugar, pop memos and find a safe pocket during
  all-hands meetings. Rematches add The Reorg, Back-to-Back Meetings,
  Synergy, then combinations. Apparently the paperwork never ends.
- **Chapter 3 and Rush Hour:** beating the Manager opens The Cup Has Gone
  Fungal and guarantees a first Mushroom Tea cup. Later cups have a quicker
  pace, with occasional spread-out Rush Hour arrivals in eligible teas.
- **Tea Tray:** temporary rewards keep upgrade offers useful when your
  spoon has exhausted its permanent upgrades.
- **Improved fortune distribution:** the reading looks at the last 16
  settled leaves, with a revised Spiral threshold. Busy cups now give the
  Crown and Star a fairer chance, while milk-heavy and concentrated play
  still shape their familiar patterns. A favored fortune is never guaranteed.
- **Earlier saved pots continue:** additive migrations retain old
  checkpoints, including saved 0.16.0 and 0.18.0 pots. Saves remain local
  to your browser and device, at completed normal-round checkpoints.

Desktop keyboard play remains the primary experience. Phone and tablet
support is best effort, and mobile testing has primarily used browser
emulation rather than physical devices.

This release is available for public tester feedback without additional
human gameplay testing. The Manager's difficulty, phone readability and
Rush Hour's feel remain open questions; Rush Hour is an initial version,
and the Manager currently shares The Daily Grind's musical arrangement.
Feedback and noncritical defects can be addressed in a later 0.19.1 patch.
