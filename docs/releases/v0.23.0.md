# Draft: 0.23.0 — Chill, Arcade and Tea Cash

Unreleased integration candidate. These notes describe the accepted local
implementation; they do not claim publication or production qualification.

- **Choose your brew:** Chill brings gentler pacing and Auto Steam; Arcade
  keeps manual firing and the full challenge. Choose before starting a pot.
  Continue Your Pot restores its own mode, with separate mode records.
- **Arcade keeps the kettle on:** every new Day carries Heat automatically,
  after the reading, upgrade and pending encounters. There is no Fresh Brew
  choice in Arcade. Chill keeps both Fresh Brew and Keep the Kettle On.
- **Seven lanes of Sugar:** each burst fires down the spoon's lane and three
  neighbours on either side.
- **Supplies worth saving:** Milk and Sugar carry between cups. Start with
  2 Milk and 1 Sugar. Chill restores 1 Milk after each completed normal cup;
  Arcade restores 1 after every 2. Sugar has no passive per-cup refill.
  Fortune, upgrade and encounter bonuses still add Milk or Sugar, but every
  award now stops at capacity.
- **Tea Cash supplies:** at a reading, once the supply bar appears and before
  choosing an upgrade, buy +1 Milk for **10 Tea Cash** or +1 Sugar for
  **25 Tea Cash** with <kbd>B</kbd> / <kbd>N</kbd> or the bar's buttons, up to
  inventory capacity. <kbd>Q</kbd> and <kbd>E</kbd> stay Milk and Sugar.
  Purchases save with the pot without adding another completed cup to the
  record book.
- **Bosses and rewards:** The Daily Grind and The Mycelium Manager use the
  supplies you carry in. Beating a milestone pays 15 Tea Cash, +1 Sugar and
  +1 Milk; beating a rematch now pays 10 Tea Cash and +1 Sugar. Against the
  Manager each sugar dart stops at the first memo, root or Manager it meets,
  and Synergy drops at most two memos per burst.
- **Sugar rules:** Sugar Rush regrows one burst every 9 seconds, at most two
  per cup. Sugar Scoop raises capacity and adds one Sugar at once. A Golden
  Cube's Sugar reward pays points instead when Sugar is full. Hot Chocolate's
  good and great grades both still earn the extra Sugar.
- **Ants between cups:** the colony can remain active around upgrade and
  restock decisions in measured safe space. Existing reduced-motion, Low
  graphics, narrow-screen and modal restrictions remain.
- **Old pots still brew:** schema 6 adds the mode. Earlier Auto Steam saves
  migrate to Chill; manual-steam saves migrate to Arcade. Migration happens
  in memory and leaves the original storage intact until the next save.
- **Research stays out of the record book:** injected development scenarios
  cannot write real mode or Other Jobs records, or overwrite/clear normal
  saved pots. Ordinary play with `?debug` remains eligible. The legacy-save
  preview now stays in memory.

The Training Cup and The Spoon's Other Jobs remain available. Desktop and
mobile Chromium checks are automated browser evidence, not physical-device
playtesting. Full integration/release qualification remains a later step.
