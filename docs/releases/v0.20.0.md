# 0.20.0 — Tiny Heroes, Big Tea Problems

**Now with More Bugs!**

The ants are intentional. The spoon's staffing request has finally been answered.

- **A little colony beside the cup:** ants wander, follow and greet each
  other, investigate crumbs and carry them home. Occasionally a team tackles
  an oversized sugar cube, with varying levels of coordination.
- **Small-print trouble:** occasional inter-round text mischief gives the
  colony something else to do. The useful reading and controls stay available.
- **A new welcome:** original caped-spoon SVG artwork and vintage tea-label
  styling bring the title screen into the adventure, with responsive controls
  and motion-aware steam.
- **Sound within reach:** press **M** or use the speaker button to mute or
  unmute music and effects together. The choice is remembered. Milk is now
  **Q / Up Arrow**; sugar remains **E / Down Arrow / S**.
- **A shade for each Day:** subtle page palettes change with in-game Days,
  repeating every seven Days, independently of real-world weekdays.

Ants and palettes are decorative, separate from gameplay and saved pots.
Ants keep clear of controls, disappear when space is too tight, pause with the
game, and are absent with reduced motion. Earlier saved pots continue.

The README now opens with the actual title screen and keeps the existing
gameplay GIF as an illustration of basic mechanics. A replacement recording,
extended human gameplay testing and physical-device testing are deferred;
automated browser emulation does not establish those results.
