# v0.17.1 — Arcade Welcome

The spoon now has a proper entrance. An interactive 80s arcade title leads
into a controls screen before gameplay, while improved focus handling makes
returning from Tea Break smoother. Gameplay mechanics remain unchanged.

- Click, tap, or press Enter to leave the title, then start or continue your
  pot from the instructions. Holding Enter cannot skip both screens.
- Returning from Tea Break restores keyboard gameplay immediately, with held
  inputs isolated across dialogs and deliberate button navigation preserved.
- Arcade buttons retain their yellow, blue and dark title palettes on hover,
  with clear keyboard focus and pressed feedback.
