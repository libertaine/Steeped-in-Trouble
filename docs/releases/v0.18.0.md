# v0.18.0 — Room for Tea

The cup has finally found some room to breathe. A larger play area, clearer
resources, a more theatrical reading of the leaves, and controls that know
when they're not needed. The spoon remains deeply underqualified.

**[▶ Play it in your browser](https://libertaine.github.io/Steeped-in-Trouble/)**

- **The cup takes center stage.** The whole cup fits your window, with the
  HUD and Pause / Menu in view, and large screens get a properly large cup.
- **A more useful HUD.** Porcelain, milk, sugar, catches and the current
  objective sit in an illustrated strip above the arena. The side
  adventures show their own resources instead.
- **The reading ceremony.** The leaves get their moment before the game
  explains what your cup did to the next fortune. The current and pending
  fortunes are labelled separately, and The Leaves Lie still lies
  convincingly. Tea Break is now four sections: Current Pot, Tea & Fortunes,
  The Spoon, and Controls & Settings.
- **Controls that know when to leave.** Tea Break → Controls & Settings →
  **On-screen controls** offers Auto, Show and Hide. On Auto, desktop
  keyboard and mouse players get the full width without touch buttons, while
  phones and tablets keep Easy Touch or Classic Touch. Your choice is
  remembered.

Saved pots from 0.17.x continue, and gameplay balance and fortune odds are
unchanged. Desktop keyboard play remains the primary experience; phone and
tablet support is best effort, and mobile behavior was checked in browser
emulation rather than on physical devices.
