# The Cup Remembers — 0.17.0 testing

**THE CUP REMEMBERS**

*The leaves remember what you did. The porcelain remembers what happened.*

0.17.0 is in development and awaits human playtesting. It adds two things to
the main game and changes nothing else: an explanation of how a round leaned
on the next fortune, and a teacup whose porcelain cracks, warns, mends with
gold, and finally breaks. The published release is still 0.16.0.

## What changed for the player

### Stage A — The Leaves Remember

Each reading now opens with **WHAT THE CUP REMEMBERS**, a short panel at the
top of the cup. Up to two observations appear in turn, each a fact about the
round and the fortune it favored:

> Milk helped defeat 4 leaves.
> *Milk-soaked leaves formed a strong Veil pattern.*

Then, at the usual reveal, the fortune card names **NEXT CUP: THE VEIL**,
says what it does in one line (*Milk clouds last 2.5 seconds longer.*), and
adds a remark. If the leaves picked something the round did not favor, it
says so: *The leaves considered your suggestion. Briefly.*

The reading's timing is unchanged (reveal at 2.1 s, upgrade offer at 2.7 s);
the memory panel appears in the first second, before the reveal. Earned
secrets join the panel in their own words (one observation makes room for
each). The Leaves Lie still claims something wonderful until it confesses;
its effect line and remark wait for the confession.

Compact View repeats the reasons and the effect as text under the cup. On
short landscape screens the steering rail, idle during a reading, gives the
text its room, and the reasons step aside once an upgrade offer needs it.
The side panel's **The cup remembers** section carries the full text for
screen readers (polite live region).

### Stage B — The Porcelain Remembers

The heart counter is replaced by the cup's condition. The simulation still
counts lives exactly as before.

| Lives | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | REINFORCED |
| 3 | CUP STABLE |
| 2 | PORCELAIN CRACKED |
| 1 | STRUCTURAL FAILURE IMMINENT |
| 0 | SHATTERED |

- A spill that costs a cup cracks the porcelain where the leaf went over.
  Strainer catches, recovering-cup rebounds, Karma's rebounds and puffs,
  near misses and kills never crack it.
- Losing a bonus fourth cup only chips the glaze (a small notch and
  hairline). Active cracks always number exactly one per cup below three.
- At one cup left: the label turns amber, a slow amber glow pulses round the
  rim (steady with reduced motion), the cracks branch further with amber
  edges, a creak plays (sound on), and the cup says *STRUCTURAL FAILURE
  IMMINENT. One more spill and the cup breaks.* A visually hidden status
  line announces each change of condition.
- **Golden Leaf:** in a normal round that starts with the cup cracked, a
  15% chance (playtesting value) of one Golden Leaf, 5–16 s in, within four
  lanes of the spoon. It sways gently in its lane for 7 s. Steam (or sugar)
  that strikes it restores one cup (never a fourth) and fills the most
  recent crack with gold. Missing it costs nothing. It scores nothing, is not
  a tea leaf, leaves no residue, and changes no fortune, tea, chapter, speed
  or upgrade. A dart that hits it counts as a hit for shot accuracy, like the
  golden and prismatic cubes; it is never a kill.
- Gold seams stay for the rest of the pot. A golden cube's extra cup and a
  Perfect Brew's restored cup also mend a crack with gold when they bring
  the cup back up to three or below; their odds and limits are unchanged.
- The last spill breaks the cup: cracks race round the rim, the porcelain
  parts into pieces, tea runs out between them, the spoon stays among the
  wreckage, and then **THE CUP HAS HAD ENOUGH.** *Management regrets to
  announce a structural failure.* Enter or **Start a fresh pot** works at
  once. With reduced motion the broken cup appears without the animation.
- A new pot starts with a whole cup.

| `?debug&cup=cracked&seed=42` | One genuine spill: one crack |
| `?debug&cup=critical&seed=42` | Two spills: critical warning |
| `?debug&cup=leaf&seed=42` | Critical, with this cup's Golden Leaf called up now |
| `?debug&cup=repair&seed=42` | One crack mended by a real steam hit on a Golden Leaf |
| `?debug&cup=seams&seed=42` | Three gold seams and one open crack |
| `?debug&cup=shatter&seed=42` | Three spills: the cup breaks |
| `?debug&cup=migrated` | Stores an authentic 0.16.0 save (critical cup, upgrade pending) before loading; choose **CONTINUE YOUR POT**. Replaces any pot saved in this browser. |

`window.__TEACUP__.cup(mode)` replays a scenario from the console. Each one
plays the simulation: leaves genuinely spill over the rim, the Golden Leaf
comes from its own stream, and repairs are steam collisions. Only waiting is
skipped (no new leaves arrive; the leaf can be called up instead of waiting
for its chance). Vite removes this code and the 0.16.0 fixture from
production builds.

## How it works

### Choosing what the cup remembers

`gameplay/memory.js` derives everything from `fortuneRecord(state)`, the same
record `readLeaves()` passes to `drawFortune()`: milk kills and uses, sugar
uses, spills, near misses, the residue lanes, and the tea's nudges. It draws
no random numbers and changes no state, so a restored reading explains itself
identically. Fortune weights and the draw are untouched.

Observations, strongest first; at most two are shown:

1. The pattern that received the +6 pattern weight: The Veil (3+ milk kills),
   The Crown (40% of leaves in three adjacent lanes) or The Spiral (10+ lanes).
2. Tactics: spills (Tempestuous Tea, +1.6 each); two or more milk pours (The
   Veil, +0.6 each), unless The Veil's pattern already said so; two or more
   sugar bursts (Sugar Rush, +1.2 each).
3. Near misses (Tempestuous Tea, +0.5 each up to four).
4. Supporting, only beside one of the above: a clean cup (The Star, Double
   Trouble and Golden Opportunity weigh more), and Herbal Tea's nudge toward
   The Leaves Lie.

The milk and sugar thresholds (`CONFIG.fortunes.memory`) choose only what is
worth mentioning. With nothing stronger than a supporting influence, one
neutral line appears instead: *A tidy cup: nothing spilled, and no strong
pattern formed.* *Quiet cups lean toward The Star and other kindly fortunes.*
That is true: The Star is the fallback pattern and a clean cup favors it.

The remark compares the drawn fortune with the fortunes the shown
observations favored. Karma, whose weight never depends on play, always gets
*Karma arrives when it pleases.* Secret conditions are never hinted at.

### Damage and repair

`state.porcelain = { serial, fractures: [{ id, kind, angle, seed }] }`
(`gameplay/porcelain.js`). `crackCup()` runs in `escape()` right after
`state.lives--`; `restoreLife()` is the single path by which any source
brings a cup back, gilding the most recent crack. A fracture's shape comes
only from its seed (derived from its serial number and angle) through a
private generator in `rendering/porcelain.js`, built once and cached. History
is capped at 14 fractures; the oldest seam or chip gives way first, never an
active crack.

The Golden Leaf (`gameplay/goldenLeaf.js`) uses its own Mulberry32 stream,
salted from the pot's seed. An eligible cup always draws twice from it
(chance, then delay); a spawned leaf draws its lane, depth and sway phase.
No other stream is touched, so fortunes, teas, leaves, bosses, adventures
and every other bonus play out exactly as before.

### Continue Pot: schema 2 and 0.16.0 saves

Checkpoints are now schema 2: the eight 0.16.0 streams plus `goldenLeaf`,
`state.porcelain`, and the always-empty `goldenLeaf` / `goldenLeafTimer`.
Validation is strict: exactly one active crack per cup below three, unique
ascending ids below `serial`, known kinds, angles in [0, 2π), uint32 seeds,
at most 14 fractures, and no extra keys.

`migrateCheckpoint()` turns a schema 1 save into schema 2 deliberately:
everything it held is kept, the eight random cursors included, so the pending
fortune, upgrade offer, Day choice and encounters come back unchanged and
nothing is rerolled. The Golden Leaf's stream starts where a fresh pot's
would. **Limitation:** 0.16.0 never recorded where its leaves spilled, so a
migrated cup's cracks (one per cup below three) are drawn at fixed, documented
places (`CONFIG.porcelain.migratedAngles`) rather than invented ones. A
migrated reinforced cup shows no chip.

Reading an old save migrates it in memory only. The stored 0.16.0 save stays
untouched until the continued pot's next checkpoint replaces it. A save that
fails migration is reported (*The saved pot could not be resumed*) and never
deleted.

## Human playtesting

Please play at least two full pots, one deliberately careless, with sound on
for part of it, and once with Compact View on a phone. Then tell us:

1. **The reading.** Before the fortune appears, do the memories make sense?
   Do they ever claim something you did not do? When the leaves pick a
   different fortune, is it clear that your play only made it more likely?
   Is it readable on your screen in Full and Compact View?
2. **A crack.** Let one leaf spill. Can you see where it went over? Does the
   crack get in the way of leaves or the spoon?
3. **Critical.** Let a second leaf spill. Is the danger unmistakable without
   counting cracks? Is the amber pulse too much, or too little? Try with
   sound off.
4. **The Golden Leaf.** Over several cracked cups, did one appear? Was it
   noticeable without dominating? Could you reach it with ordinary steering?
   Is 15% about right? (The `?debug&cup=leaf` shortcut helps in development.)
5. **Gold.** After a repair, does the seam read as a mended crack? After a
   long pot, does the cup tell its history?
6. **The break.** Lose the last cup. Is the shattering satisfying and short
   enough? Does Enter start a new pot straight away? Try with reduced motion.
7. **An old save.** If you have a 0.16.0 pot saved in this browser, continue
   it. Does it return to the same reading and upgrade, with cracks for any
   lost cups?
8. **Nothing else changed.** Do combat, fortunes, upgrades, bosses, the
   adventures, the Arcade Collection and scoring feel exactly as before?

Release remains gated on this feedback. Cracked lanes, gold seam bonuses and
any other effect of damage on play are deliberately not part of 0.17.0.
