# 0.20.0: Quick audio toggle

Baseline: `0a9bec595ac25c476f234b8d31dff077ebe828bb`, on
`feature/0.20.0-tiny-heroes-big-tea-problems`.

**Status:** implemented for review. No version bump, merge or release.

## Decision: M moves from milk to sound

M was a third milk key (with Q and ↑) and was documented as one. Asked before
the change, the owner chose to move milk off M. Milk is now Q or ↑; sugar
keeps E, ↓ and S. Tests that pressed M for milk now press Q.

## What changed

- **One switch.** `audio.enabled` remains the only sound state. The Settings
  "Sound" toggle, three speaker buttons and M all flip it through
  `ui/soundToggle.js`. There are no separate music or effects mutes and no
  new volume controls.
- **Immediate, clean muting.** Everything plays through a master gain, so
  muting silences music and effects at once and drops the effects already
  scheduled. Unmuting restores the master and lets the band fade back in.
  It reuses the same AudioContext and band, keeps their volumes, and plays
  no chirp. The old confirmation beep is removed.
- **Autoplay.** No AudioContext exists before a user gesture. A saved "on"
  unlocks on the first key press, click or tap. A muted player never creates
  one.
- **Saved choice.** `steeped-in-trouble:sound` is `on` or `off`. Missing or
  unknown values read as off, which was the previous default, so existing
  players hear no change. Storage errors are ignored. Gameplay saves and the
  other preferences are untouched.
- **Placement.** The title speaker sits at the label's top right, under the
  imprint rule. On an upright phone it sits in the centred row after
  Graphics. The game's speaker is in the page header, before View, so it is
  available in play, pauses, readings and between rounds. The Tea Break's
  speaker sits beside Back to cup / Resume game. Each is a 44 × 44 px native
  button with an inline SVG speaker (waves when on, a cross when muted).
  Each has an "Mute audio" / "Unmute audio" label and a tooltip naming M.
  The instruction overlay, the collection and the pot confirmation have no
  speaker; M works over all of them.
- **Keyboard.** M toggles once per press, everywhere. It ignores auto-repeat,
  Ctrl, Alt, Meta and Shift, IME composition, and focused text fields,
  selects and editable content. On the title screen, Enter and Space on the
  focused speaker activate it and never advance onboarding. Held Enter is
  still blocked until keyup. A pointer click on the header speaker returns
  focus to the cup, so the next Space is steam.
- **Controls reference.** "Mute / Unmute Audio — M" is in Controls &
  Settings and the README. The instruction overlay folds M into its Pause
  row ("Pause · Mute / unmute", P · M), so the overlay still fits a
  375 × 667 window.

## Tests

- `tests/unit/audio.test.js`: no context before a gesture; master routing;
  muting silences and schedules nothing; unmuting makes no sound, reuses the
  context and band, and keeps the band's level; the simulation is unchanged.
  It also checks a browser without Web Audio.
- `tests/unit/soundToggle.test.js`: the M rules; single counting; speaker
  and Settings labels; header focus hand-back; persistence and bad values;
  unlock listeners.
- `tests/browser/soundToggle.spec.js`: M across title, instructions, cup,
  pause and reading, with milk and sugar keys unaffected. Also: pointer,
  Enter and Space on the speakers; the Tea Break; form fields; reloads;
  gesture-gated AudioContext creation, counted; blur and visibility.
- `tests/browser/mobile-sound-toggle.spec.js`: tap targets, in-viewport and
  non-overlap checks in portrait and landscape.
- `tests/browser/titlePackaging.js`: the speaker joins the title fit and
  focus-wrap checks.
