# 0.20.0: In-game Day background palettes

Baseline: `a43c953f745fdd940146d05958b8b21ad2087141`, on
`feature/0.20.0-tiny-heroes-big-tea-problems`.

**Status:** implemented for review. No version bump, merge or release.

This replaces the earlier "seven local-day backgrounds" idea in
`v0.20.0-ant-colony.md`. The palettes follow the **in-game Day** (ten normal
cups each), not the player's weekday, clock or timezone.

## Design decision: a dark ground, tinted

The brief suggested light cream grounds. The gameplay page is dark, and its
header, kicker, version pill and footer lettering are light (`#f1dfbd`,
`#a1bdb0`, `#93a99b`). A cream page would put that text at about 1.0–1.9:1
contrast (the brand would vanish entirely). Making it readable again would mean recolouring the header and
footer, which is outside this increment.

So each Day keeps a dark ground and shifts its hue: a change of ambient light
rather than a new room. The suggested cream becomes the Day's **paper** colour.
It tints the fine rule under the header and names the palette. Day 1 is the
established page ground, colour for colour, so a first Day looks exactly as
before.

If a light-paper page is wanted later, it needs a header and footer restyle
for contrast. That is a separate, reviewable change.

## Behaviour

- **Source of truth:** `paletteForRound(state.round)` goes through
  `gameplay/days.js` `dayOf()`, the same Day as the HUD. There is no new
  state, save field or schema change. Invalid or missing rounds use Day 1.
- **When it changes:** on the Day choice. The tenth cup's reading and the
  "Day N complete" screen still show Day N. Cup 11 starts on Day N+1.
- **Fade:** about 0.8 s, a CSS transition of registered `--day-*` colours.
  It runs only for a Day choice on High graphics. There is no JS animation,
  timer, delay or input block.
- **Snaps:** a new pot, Continue, a reload, Low graphics, debug set-ups,
  and `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (live changes included).
- **Arcade Collection practice:** practice pots are round 1, so they show
  Day 1's ground. Returning to the main pot snaps back to its Day.
- **Untouched:** playfield and canvas, cup and tea, HUD pills and resource
  bar, side panels, buttons and focus rings, reading/upgrade/Day choice cards,
  Tea Break, overlays, ants, and the Concept B title dialog, which covers the
  page with its own colours.

## Files

- `src/ui/dayPalette.js`: palette table, selection and the `<body>` controller.
- `src/main.js`: one controller, fed events and updated after the HUD.
- `styles/main.css`: `@property` registrations (Day 1 initial values), the
  body gradient, the header rule and the `data-day-fade` transition.
- `tests/unit/dayPalette.test.js`: selection, cycle, fallbacks, contrast, CSS
  defaults, fade/snap rules, real Day choice and new pot, and real
  checkpoints, with simulation and RNG unchanged.
- `tests/browser/dayPalette.spec.js` and `mobile-day-palette.spec.js`: computed
  `--day-*` values and backgrounds, untouched element colours, mid-fade
  values, reduced motion and Low graphics, Continue across two reloads, New
  Pot, the title, 1920×1080 / 1280×720 / 800×600, phone portrait and
  landscape, tablet, and a tapped Day choice.
