# Crossing the Street screenshots

Production-build captures from the 0.15.0 browser tests (seed 42), opened
and inspected for legibility, clipping, control placement, whether the
safe areas and traffic directions read without explanation, and whether
speech bubbles or cards cover the traffic or the spoon during play.
CSS-pixel scale; browser emulation is not hardware testing.

Observations: the sidewalks, median and lane directions read at every size;
vehicles differ by silhouette (short rounded car, boxy van with a cab, long
spotted tanker) as well as colour. On phones the canvas is small and the
spoon is drawn a little larger; scenery stays plain so the spoon, traffic
and shop come first. Titles shrink to fit their cards.
