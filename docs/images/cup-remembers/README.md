Chromium, seed 42 unless noted), opened and inspected for legibility,
clipping, whether cracks stay out of the way of leaves and the spoon, and
whether the condition reads without counting cracks. CSS-pixel scale;
browser emulation is not hardware testing.

Observations: the memory panel sits above the leaf glyph and the fortune
card below it, both readable in Full View; on phones the canvas text is
small, so Compact View repeats the reasons and the effect under the cup.
Cracks stay on the porcelain band and the rim's edge, beneath the spoon and
the leaves; they are subtle while the cup is merely cracked and branch with
amber edges when critical. Gold seams read as mended cracks. The condition
pill fits every size, including the long STRUCTURAL FAILURE IMMINENT. The
shattered cup keeps the spoon on the rim and the message inside the cup.
The mid-break capture sets the cosmetic break clock; everything else is
captured as played.
