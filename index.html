Finish a round to read the leaves. Your tactics become the next round's rules.

Tea leaves from defeated foes collect in the cup. At the end of each round they form a reading that changes the next round. How you played sways the reading, but the leaves have opinions of their own.

Hold STEAM to fire continuously, or turn on Auto Steam to fire at whatever lines up with the spoon. Milk washes the leaves in its wedge back into the cup and slows them; sugar shoots three strong crystalline darts. The tea strainer catches the first leaf to escape each cup.

On a touch screen, Easy Touch gives you two big steering pads and Auto Steam: double-tap the cup to pour milk, and hold a finger on it to fire sugar. Classic Touch keeps a button for everything, and works with Auto Steam too.

Original teacup concept; all artwork procedurally drawn with HTML Canvas paths. No arcade-game assets, fonts, or external libraries.
